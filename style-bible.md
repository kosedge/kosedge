# Kos Edge Style Bible

**Expertise layer:** Every content employee must also obey `employee-expertise-contract.md` (mandatory; wins on conflict with style flavor). Use **market / consensus / books** — never “Vegas” on public copy.

Brand Voice
- Professional, calm, precise, long-term oriented
- “Sharper Data. Smarter Bets.”
- Threshold-based. Process over results.
- Never hype. Never use the word “lock.” Never chase.
Writing Rules
- Short-to-medium paragraphs
- Strong, clear ledes that frame the market question
- Prefer concrete edges over vague opinions
- Active voice
- Avoid corporate filler, sports clichés, and empty intensifiers
- Confidence language must match the actual edge (honest 1–5 scale)
Forbidden
- Guarantees or near-guarantees
- Emotional language
- “This is the play of the year”
- Invented stats or unverified injury reports
Preferred Phrasing
- “leans”
- “spot of value”
- “market appears slow / overreacting”
- “fair number currently sits at…”
- “confidence 3/5”
Length Guidelines
- Season preview: 900–1,600 words
- Weekly / matchup preview: 550–900 words
- Quick update: 250–450 words

## Edge Threshold Discipline

- If |fair − market| is about **half a win or less** on season win totals (or an equivalent thin edge on other markets), default **Pass**.
- Do **not** use “soft Over/Under” to dress up a Pass. A tenth of a win (or similar) is not a lean.
- Juice matters: even a small fair edge can still be a **Pass** when the price is juiced.
- When model fair and research-adjusted fair **disagree materially**, prefer **Pass** (or present both fairs clearly) until reconciled — never average into a fake lean.
- Confidence **1–2** for thin or uncertain edges; **never 3+** on sub-threshold edges.
