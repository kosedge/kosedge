# Las Vegas Raiders 2026 Season Preview

**By Sam Ortiz** · Kos Edge Analytics · AFC West desk  
**Angle:** Reset year — noise ≠ edge  
**Date:** August 17, 2026
**Sources (beat desk):** Ryan McFadden (ESPN) @ryanmcfadden_; Tashan Reed (The Athletic) @tashanreed; Vincent Bonsignore (Las Vegas Review-Journal) @VinnyBonsignore  
**Market (DK/RotoWire, late July 2026):** Season wins 5.5 (−146 / +120)

Las Vegas changed the head coach, the quarterback room, and a chunk of the offensive line. The internet treated that like a betting edge. The board treated it like a price move: **5.5** with Over −146 and Under +120.

That juice is the story. A 3-14 team should open with Under money everywhere. By late July the Over is the expensive side. The market already bought the reset. Your job is not to buy it again at −146 because Fernando Mendoza won the Heisman or because Maxx Crosby is back in silver and black after a trade that fell apart.

Noise is loud in Henderson. Edge is scarce.

Ryan McFadden’s ESPN camp preview framed the spring correctly: new coach, Crosby back after the Baltimore detour collapsed, Tyler Linderbaum money on the interior, Mendoza vs. Cousins as the gravitational story. Tashan Reed and Vincent Bonsignore will own the daily QB-rep and Crosby participation notes. Use them for Week 1 markets. Do not let them talk you into −146 on a number the books already flipped.

## What camp actually clarified

Klint Kubiak, with veterans reporting, removed the fake mystery: **Kirk Cousins begins camp as the starting quarterback and will get a ton of reps**. Mendoza and Aidan O’Connell are there to push him. That matches Kubiak’s spring preference — sit the No. 1 pick early if the veteran can hold the fort.

AP and Review-Journal coverage (Bonsignore’s shop) have Mendoza working his way up after third-team spring snaps. Kubiak’s evaluation language is about adversity response and mistake correction, not completion percentage theater. That is developmental honesty. It is also a reminder that “Mendoza szn” tweets are not a win-total input until the depth chart says so.

Crosby has publicly tracked ahead of schedule from offseason knee surgery; Kubiak’s camp comments have him expected to practice. Kwity Paye and Malcolm Koonce give the edge room real depth if Crosby’s snap management is real. That helps the defense’s floor. It does not turn six division games against Denver, Kansas City, and the Chargers into value.

Ashton Jeanty and Brock Bowers remain the skill pieces that make a Cousins-led drive functional. Linderbaum plus Kolton Miller plus Jackson Powers-Johnson is a better OL conversation than 2025’s sack disaster. Better is not the same as priced. The Over juice says the market already moved off the floor.

## Schedule is the Under’s friend, not a hammer

Six games inside an AFC West where the other three win totals sit at 9.5, 10.5, and 9.5 is a structural cap. Primetime Raiders games will attract public Overs and side money that has nothing to do with EPA. Early-season Unders in new-scheme spots can be live on a week-to-week board. That is a different product from season win totals.

If Mendoza overtakes Cousins in October, variance explodes both ways — rookies create wins you did not model and losses you cannot fade after the fact. Wide error bars are why reset years produce fewer season-long leans, not more.

## What the market may be wrong about

Any national “they’re back” piece that skips the division tax and the −146. The market may also be slightly slow if Cousins plus Kubiak plus Crosby produces a clean 7–8 win climb — but that thesis wants a better number or plus-money Over, not chalk on 5.5. Under at +120 is only interesting if camp shows Crosby setbacks *and* the OL looks like 2025 again. That is not the current evidence.
