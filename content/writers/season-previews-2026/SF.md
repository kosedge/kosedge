## Availability is the scheme

Kyle Shanahan’s offense remains a structural edge when the chain is intact: Christian McCaffrey, George Kittle, Brock Purdy, and Mike Evans as a contested-catch stabilizer. Excellence is already partly in the number when the board asks for ten wins; it is less overpriced than when shops asked for eleven.

Camp reality late July (Wagoner / Tafur / Chronicle): Ricky Pearsall (knee swelling / PCL concern), Christian Kirk (calf, out indefinitely early), Nick Bosa (ACL recovery, individual work, phased return aiming at Week 1), Vederian Lowe (high-ankle), Malik Mustapha (hamstring), Jordan James (rib), and Shanahan limited after a July 14 car accident with Klay Kubiak on the headset. None of those lines alone kills a season. The pattern does.
