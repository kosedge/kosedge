# Pittsburgh Steelers 2026 Season Preview

**By Reese Quinn** · Kos Edge Analytics · AFC North desk
**Angle:** Steelers football as a totals market first — pace, possession, and QB durability — with starter certainty that still isn’t season certainty
**Sources (beat desk):** Brooke Pryor (ESPN) @bepryor; Mark Kaboly (The Athletic / Pat McAfee) @MarkKaboly; Dale Lolley (Steelers.com / DK) @dlolley_pgh

Pittsburgh won the AFC North at 10-7 last season and the 2026 win total came back at **8.5**. Late July DraftKings / RotoWire price that number roughly **Over +100 / Under -120**. Division champions are not supposed to open a win and a half below what they just did unless the market thinks the wins were rented. Our desk agrees with the direction of that skepticism — and we start from totals logic, not from nostalgia about black-and-gold floor narratives.

Kos Edge / KEICMB (2026-07-29) projects Pittsburgh at **7.57 expected wins**, with playoff odds near **25%** and division title odds under **8%**. That is a full win under the number before you even open a camp notebook. Research does not erase that gap; camp notes mostly explain why the sim is cold on a 42-year-old quarterback in a new-old coaching marriage.

## Totals market first

Mike Tomlin is gone. Mike McCarthy is in — Pittsburgh native, longtime Rodgers head coach in Green Bay, more recently the Cowboys’ sideline. Aaron Rodgers re-signed for what he has now repeated with “zero debate” finality: 2026 is the last season (Pryor / NFL.com report-day coverage). The reunion sells. The playing style is the bet.

McCarthy’s recent offenses have not been possession factories in the modern sense. Slower average seconds-per-snap profiles limit how many cracks a middling offense gets to outscore its own variance. Steelers football under Tomlin often lived in low-event, one-score games; Pittsburgh went **7-3 in one-score contests** in 2025 by multiple recaps. That style can pad a win total when turnover luck lands. It can also vanish when the same team meets a harder division and a schedule that does not gift close games.

So the win-total question is not “will Pittsburgh be competitive?” Competitive is the brand. The question is whether a McCarthy-Rodgers offense generates enough efficient possessions — and whether the defense still finishes enough of the rest — to clear nine wins against Baltimore and Cincinnati versions that look meaner on paper than last year’s North.

Game totals and team totals will often be the sharper expression of this roster than sides. If Pittsburgh walks the ball down the field and wins 17–13, Unders and low team totals will have a season-long friend. If Rodgers still has enough arm to stress DK Metcalf and Michael Pittman Jr. vertically while the offensive line protects, the same club becomes a live dog that covers. Start from the number of snaps and the age of the quarterback. Work outward to the division race.

## Latrobe: starter locked, season not

Brooke Pryor’s ESPN camp hub out of Saint Vincent College has the hierarchy clear: Rodgers is QB1; Mason Rudolph opened as the primary backup with second-team reps; Will Howard and rookie Drew Allar split the third group. Mark Kaboly’s practice tracker notes match McCarthy’s own framing — Rodgers on a deliberate early-camp **rep count**, roughly **40–50%** of snaps in week one, with Rudolph first in line when Rodgers is done. That is not a competition for the starting job. It is an admission that a 42-year-old arm is a managed asset.

Rodgers’ Day 1 work was sharp in short samples (clean early completions, including to free-agent addition Brandon Smith), and the deep ball to Metcalf drew a pass breakup from Asante Samuel Jr. in two-minute work — useful color, not a futures pivot. What does matter for 8.5: Joey Porter Jr. and Jalen Ramsey opened on the **PUP list**, bumping Samuel’s rep volume. A Steelers defense that loses two high-end corners for any stretch of the regular season becomes a different totals team overnight. Track activation timelines through Pryor and Lolley’s team-site notes before you treat Pittsburgh’s defensive identity as last year’s unit.

Skill-room noise has been constructive. Rodgers and Metcalf both praised Roman Wilson’s spring tape after a late-2025 healthy-scratch stretch; Pittman has already bought into McCarthy’s situational conditioning emphasis. Those are positive process notes for an offense searching for reliable third options behind the names everyone knows. They do not override the age cliff or the PUP list.

## Why Under is the lean — and why it isn’t free

| Input | Read |
| --- | --- |

| KEICMB mean | 7.57 expected wins |
| Research adjusters | Rodgers durability, McCarthy pace, Porter/Ramsey PUP, harder North |
| Research-adjusted fair | ~7.4–7.8 |
| Edge | ~0.7–1.1 wins to the Under before juice |

A full win of model edge usually clears threshold. Juice at -120 is payable if you believe the fair. The key risk is the Steelers’ organizational habit of clearing these numbers anyway — six straight seasons of beating the win total in some market histories, and a franchise allergy to losing records that makes Unders feel cursed. Process still wins: last year’s 10 wins included close-game clustering and a North that was softer at the bottom. Baltimore under Minter and Cincinnati with Burrow healthy are not the same division opponents.

Over reopen conditions: Porter and Ramsey back for Week 1; Rudolph never needed; offensive line keeps Rodgers clean; Pittsburgh steals the same one-score diet. Until those stack, the Under is the side that matches both the sim and the age curve.

## Betting Guide

- **Win total:** Lean **Under 8.5** at -120 or better. If Under moves to -140, shrink to Pass — same thesis, worse price.
- **Game totals:** Prefer Unders in Pittsburgh home spots that project as McCarthy grinders, especially if Porter/Ramsey are out or limited.
- **Sides:** Neutral-to-fade Steelers as road favorites against ascending offenses; live dogs at home remain the brand’s best shape.
- **Props:** Metcalf/Pittman receiving is healthier exposure than Rodgers season pass yards until the rep-count philosophy and preseason snap plan clarify. Avoid blind Overs on Steelers team totals in September.
