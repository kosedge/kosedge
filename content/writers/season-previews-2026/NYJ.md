# New York Jets 2026 Season Preview

**By Taylor Brooks** · Kos Edge Analytics · NFC/AFC desk
**Angle:** Defense-led team searching for offensive floor
**Sources (beat desk):** Rich Cimini (ESPN) @RichCimini; Zack Rosenblatt (The Athletic) @ZackBlatt; Brian Costello (New York Post) @BrianCoz

The Jets’ season turns on whether the offense can stay above replacement while the defense remains a top-unit bet. That sounds simple. Betting it is not.

## The number that matters

Win totals that assume ‘defense travels’ still need an offensive floor. Rich Cimini and Zack Rosenblatt reporting on QB competition and OL health should set your prior.

## Roster & scheme

If the Jets win, it will be short fields and explosive defense. If they lose, it will be stalled drives and negative EPA on early downs.

## Schedule & spots

Prime-time games inflate totals; Jets games often want the Under until offense proves otherwise.

## What the market may be wrong about

Narratives about ‘turning the corner’ ignore that corner requires sustained offensive success rate.

## Betting Guide

- Win total: Pass — two-way variance is high.
- Totals: Lean Under early unless pace data flips.
- Sides: Prefer Jets as dogs with defensive identity intact.
