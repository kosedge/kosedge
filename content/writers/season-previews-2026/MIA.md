# Miami Dolphins 2026 Season Preview

**By Taylor Brooks** · Kos Edge Analytics · NFC/AFC desk
**Angle:** Explosive offense vs fragility
**Sources (beat desk):** Marcel Louis-Jacques (ESPN) @Marcel_LJ; David Furones (Sun Sentinel) @DavidFurones_; Joe Schad (Palm Beach Post) @schadjoe

Miami’s preview is a durability problem dressed up as a scheme story. The Dolphins can still stress any defense vertically when healthy; the betting question is how many weeks that version shows up.

## The number that matters

Win totals price hope for another high-octane year. Beat notes from Marcel Louis-Jacques and David Furones on OT/IOL availability should move your fair number more than training-camp hype clips.

## Roster & scheme

Tyreek Hill / Jaylen Waddle dynamics still define explosive rate. If either role compresses, the entire total and team Over softens. QB play must stay clean under pressure — Miami’s pressure-allowed rate is the quiet killshot.

## Schedule & spots

Early-season heat games and London/travel spots matter. Do not treat every home game as an automatic pace-up.

## What the market may be wrong about

Markets often buy ‘talent’ and sell ‘availability.’ Miami is the poster child.

## Betting Guide

- Win total: Pass unless juice offers a clear Under after OL injuries.
- Sides: Prefer undersized favorites against Miami when key skill pieces are questionable.
- Props: Waddle targets more stable than highlight-dependent deep shots.
