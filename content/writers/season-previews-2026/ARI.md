# Arizona Cardinals 2026 Season Preview: Win Total 7.5

**By Morgan Hale** · Kos Edge Analytics · NFC West desk  
**Angle:** Bridge-QB ceiling vs a 7.5 the desert roster has not earned  
**Sources (beat desk):** Josh Weinfuss (ESPN) [@joshweinfuss](https://x.com/joshweinfuss); Bo Brack (PHNX Sports) [@BoBrack](https://x.com/BoBrack); Darren Urban (AZCardinals.com) [@Cardschatter](https://x.com/Cardschatter)  
**Market (primary late July 2026):** Win total **7.5** (shop juice live)  
**Research stamp:** 2026-07-30 ET  

Arizona is not priced as a basement club anymore. That is the problem.

The Cardinals released Kyler Murray after a 3-14 season that ended Jonathan Gannon’s tenure. What remains is a first-year Mike LaFleur offense, a bridge quarterback who missed most of the spring install, and a regenerating skill group in the league’s hardest division. The market question at **7.5** is no longer “can they clear four?” It is whether situational football and Marvin Harrison Jr. volume can invent eight wins against Los Angeles, Seattle, and San Francisco six times a year. This desk’s answer: the board got ahead of the bridge.

Kos Edge / KEINFL (2026-07-29) puts Arizona at **5.03 expected wins**, ~1.7% playoff, near-zero Super Bowl equity. That is a reconstruction number sitting next to a mid-pack total.

## The primary number — a different bet than the old basement boards

Late-July consensus this desk is arguing hangs Arizona at **7.5**. Older midsummer boards floated 3.5 / 4.5; those are different tickets if they still exist. Do not import an old Over lean onto 7.5.

Against **7.5**, research-adjusted fair near **4.7–5.4** is roughly two-plus wins Under. Lean **Under 7.5**. Confidence **2/5** because soft non-division spots exist and LaFleur’s scheme can spike for a month. **Pass** if Under juice worsens past −140.

## Bridge QB, not franchise QB

Josh Weinfuss’s camp file is the primary desk feed. Jacoby Brissett held out of voluntary work, then agreed to roughly **$15.5M guaranteed** on a one-year rework (ESPN / Schefter; AZCardinals.com / Urban). LaFleur wants Brissett with the ones toward a Week 1 start at the Chargers. Missed OTAs compress timing with Harrison, Michael Wilson, and Trey McBride under a new call sheet. Gardner Minshew and rookie Carson Beck create contingency noise, not a reason to invent eight wins of bridge efficiency.

The old Murray ceiling is **not** the 2026 bet. Brissett efficiency in structure, Love/Allgeier/Conner/Benson committee noise, and McBride as the most reliable target support props. They do not support a 7.5 Over.

## Roster levers and schedule tax

Arizona drafted Jeremiyah Love No. 3 overall and signed Tyler Allgeier while still carrying James Conner and Trey Benson. Early-down success rate moves wins more than August headlines. Paris Johnson Jr. opened camp on PUP — monitor Week 1. Six NFC West games against Rams/Seahawks/49ers are the tax. Steal weeks exist; they are not a process edge for eight wins.

Local ceiling talk still frames anything over five as a surprise and seven as the optimistic extreme. That brackets fair near five — and frames **7.5** as a hopeful hang.
