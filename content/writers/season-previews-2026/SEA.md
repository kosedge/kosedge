Seattle is the defending Super Bowl champion. That sentence sells tickets. It does not, by itself, clear a win-total Over — unless the board has already cut deep enough that nine wins is the ask.

The Seahawks’ betting identity for 2026 is structural on defense and unsettled on offensive process. Mike Macdonald’s unit led the league in scoring defense in 2025, punctuated a Super Bowl with pressure and takeaways, and has shown up again in early Renton camp as the side winning red-zone periods (Henderson / Condotta / Bell). Sam Darnold is still the starter. The questions around him — and around what the offense looks like without Klint Kubiak and without the same run-game spine — are why higher boards were a debate. At **10.5**, those questions still exist. They no longer have to invent an eleven-win season to cash. Older **10.5** boards are a different ticket if they still exist — Pass or re-price; do not import this Over lean.

## Defense is the prior

Start where the tape and the camp notes agree. Seattle’s front and coverage structure create negative plays without needing heroic offense. Early camp: unblocked blitzes that would be sacks in games, red-zone stops to close practice, rookies generating takeaways on backup periods. Gregg Bell and Bob Condotta’s local notebooks track the same theme — the defending unit has not softened its identity.

That prior supports a playoff-floor team. At 10.5 it did not automatically support eleven wins. At **10.5** it supports nine more cleanly: complementary football still matters, but the ticket no longer requires peak 2025 scoring luck for seventeen weeks.

## The QB and offense questions the number must price

Darnold delivered a title run. He also threw interceptions as part of the package. Henderson’s camp updates show Darnold working with Rashid Shaheed, Jaxon Smith-Njigba, Tory Horton, and first-round back Jadarian Price — the skill group is not empty. The process flags are elsewhere:

- **Coordinator change** — Kubiak is gone; Brian Fleury’s first year as OC is an efficiency variance input  
- **Run-game attrition** — Kenneth Walker III is out of the building; Zach Charbonnet’s knee has him on a delayed track; Price is earning passing-game trust in camp but is still a rookie workload bet  
- **Health mean** — 2025’s win total rode unusually clean availability for Darnold, JSN, and key OL pieces. That is not a contractual guarantee  
- **Backup noise** — Jalen Milroe’s camp swings matter for contingency, not for naming a Week 1 controversy  

Northwest December weather remains a totals story, not a summer slogan. It helps Unders in ugly scripts. It does not decide 8.5 alone — and it is a thinner Under argument when the market only asks for nine.

## Division and schedule without the parade

Rams added Garrett and McDuffie. 49ers remain a weekly problem when healthy. Arizona is the division’s win bank — and even those are no longer automatic freebies if LaFleur’s offense spikes for a month. Seattle’s path to nine still exists through home dominance and defensive floor. The path to seven exists through a quarter of negative offensive scripts and a couple of lost one-scores. Championship hangover is a cliché until you look at how many defending titles regress a win or two while remaining good. The market already cut Seattle from a 14-win season to **8.5**. Paying even money-ish to say they still get to nine is closer to buying the structure than buying the logo.
