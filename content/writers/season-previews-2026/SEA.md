Seattle is the defending Super Bowl champion. That sentence sells tickets. It does not, by itself, clear a win-total Over — unless the board has already cut deep enough that nine wins is the ask.

The Seahawks’ betting identity for 2026 is structural on defense and unsettled on offensive process. Mike Macdonald’s unit led the league in scoring defense in 2025 and has shown up again in early Renton camp as the side winning red-zone periods (Henderson / Condotta / Bell). Sam Darnold is still the starter. The questions around him — and around what the offense looks like without Klint Kubiak and without the same run-game spine — are why higher boards were a debate. At **8.5**, those questions still exist. They no longer have to invent an eleven-win season to cash.

## Defense is the prior

Seattle’s front and coverage structure create negative plays without needing heroic offense. Early camp notes track unblocked blitzes, red-zone stops, and rookies generating takeaways on backup periods. That prior supports a playoff-floor team. At 10.5 it did not automatically support eleven wins. At **8.5** it supports nine more cleanly: complementary football still matters, but the ticket no longer requires peak 2025 scoring luck for seventeen weeks.

## The QB and offense questions the number must price

Darnold delivered a title run. He also threw interceptions as part of the package. Henderson’s camp updates show Darnold working with Rashid Shaheed, Jaxon Smith-Njigba, Tory Horton, and first-round back Jadarian Price — the skill group is not empty. Process flags: coordinator change (Brian Fleury’s first year as OC), run-game attrition (Kenneth Walker III out; Zach Charbonnet delayed; Price still a rookie workload bet), and health mean-reversion after unusually clean 2025 availability. Northwest December weather helps Unders in ugly scripts; it is a thinner Under argument when the market only asks for nine.
