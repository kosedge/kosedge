# Buffalo Bills 2026 Season Preview

**By Taylor Brooks** · Kos Edge Analytics · NFC/AFC desk
**Angle:** QB ceiling vs AFC East arms race
**Sources (beat desk):** Alaina Getzenberg (ESPN) @agetzenberg; Joe Buscaglia (The Athletic) @JoeBuscaglia; Matt Parrino (Syracuse.com) @MattParrino

Buffalo’s 2026 win-total question is not whether Josh Allen can still create explosive drives. It is whether the supporting cast and defensive continuity can keep the Bills in the narrow band where elite QB play turns into January home games.

## The number that matters

Market pricing still treats Buffalo as an AFC East favorite with Super Bowl equity. That is fair as a brand narrative. As a betting number, it only clears if the offensive line and skill-usage rates look like 2024 peak rather than midseason drift. Watch early-down success and red-zone TD rate in camp reports from Alaina Getzenberg and Joe Buscaglia — those are leading indicators the board often lags.

## Roster & scheme

Allen remains the lever. The supporting cast must prove it can win without forcing him into hero-ball third-and-longs. Defensively, the Bills need pressure generation without selling out coverage integrity; AFC East offenses will test both edges weekly.

## Schedule & spots

Division games decide the over/under more than the national slate. Short weeks and cold-weather home games still help — but only if the OL is healthy in November.

## What the market may be wrong about

Public will overweight Super Bowl odds and underweight OL attrition. If camp notes flag right-side protection issues, the win-total Over becomes a Pass even if the brand still sells.

## Betting Guide

- Win total: lean under only if OL/camp red flags stack; else Pass on thin juice.
- Side theme: fade Buffalo as a huge favorite on the road in September until usage stabilizes.
- Prop theme: Allen rush attempts — role clarity over raw ceiling.
