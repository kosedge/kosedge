Rip City’s summer was Dame coming home and Ja Morant walking in the door. Opening night is a 36-year-old post-Achilles point guard, a 26-year-old who played 20 games last year, and no Shaedon Sharpe.

Micah Nori got the job June 23 on a one-year plus two team options (Oregonian). First head-coaching job, from Finch’s bench. June 29 they sent Jerami Grant and Kris Murray to Memphis for Morant (NBA.com). Robert Williams III signed a three-year, $44 million extension as the backup center. Matisse Thybulle left for the Lakers July 27. Duop Reath was already gone in February.

Sharpe tore the right lateral meniscus in an offseason workout, had surgery August 24, and the club said August 28 he is out at least six months (Oregonian; OPB/AP). That is an opener inactive. Damian Lillard missed all of 2025-26. Nori has said he starts and closes if he can walk (Jason Quick, The Athletic). Expected back October 21 versus Phoenix. Six of the first eight are on the road, 18 of the first 31 away (Oregonian).

Street is 43.5 on last year’s 42-win club. The engine nine is not this roster. Pass.
