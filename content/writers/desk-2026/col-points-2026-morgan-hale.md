The Avalanche’s 5-on-5 expected-goal gap last season was a Presidents’ Trophy team. The actual-goal gap was a different sport. Books split the difference, posted 108.5 on an 84-game slate, and juiced the over. The mismatch is goaltending variance sitting on an intact Bednar/Makar system — not a 121-point repeat that never belonged on this board.

Official ledger, 2025-26: 55-16-11, 121 points, Central and Presidents’ Trophy, NHL-best 298 goals for and 197 against (Coby Maeir, ColoradoAvalanche.com; Hockey-Reference). Penalty kill 84.62 percent, first. Power play 17.11 percent, a bottom-tier rate on a team that led the league at even strength. Nathan MacKinnon 53-74-127 in 80 games. Martin Necas 38-62-100 in 78. Cale Makar 20-59-79 in 75. Scott Wedgewood 31-6-6, .921, 2.02 in 45 games (43 starts). Mackenzie Blackwood 23-10-2, .904, 2.51 in 39 (36 starts). The tandem took the Jennings; the 203-goals-against figure includes shootout deciders (Jon Lane, NHL.com).

Process, not the 121, is the input. Hockey-Reference 5-on-5: Corsi for 57.5 percent. Expected goals 211.6 for, 157.5 against. Actual 5-on-5 goals 216 and 128. Shooting 9.0 percent against a league 8.7. Save percentage .927 against a league .913. PDO 101.7. Scott Maxwell at Daily Faceoff (Aug. 27): Colorado tied Carolina for the NHL lead in 5-on-5 expected goals for per 60 (3.33) and ranked fourth in expected goals against (2.50) — the only club in the top five both ways. Five-on-five shooting percentage 9.65 percent, 16th. The offense was real. The goal-prevention on the scoresheet ran well ahead of the chances.

That gap is Wedgewood. Hockey-Reference goals saved above average: 28.3 for Wedgewood, 7.5 for Blackwood. A 33-year-old career year, then a .877 save percentage on 73 shots in the first three games of a four-game Western Conference Final sweep (Lane). Evan Rawal (Denver Gazette, July 9) reported the club still wants Blackwood as the 1 in an 84-game tandem; Joe Sakic said Blackwood is healthy after missing camp a year ago. Miner and Ilya Nabokov sit behind them. The plan is not a star import. The plan is last year’s pair with a starter’s share that likely moves toward the 29-year-old. Regression in net is not a narrative. It is the base rate.

The blue line is the system that survives that regression. Makar, Devon Toews, Josh Manson, Brent Burns (one-year), Brett Kulak (five-year), Sam Malinski. Makar’s eight-year, $163.2 million extension (AAV $20.4 million, 2027-28 start; NHL.com/AP/The Athletic, Aug. 28–29) does not hit this cap. Up front, the subtraction is the price of last spring’s deadline. Valeri Nichushkin (17-32-49 in 72) to Columbus for picks (Rawal, June 25). Ross Colton and Jack Drury to Nashville; Fedor Svechkov and Zachary L’Heureux back the other way, plus Jaden Schwartz on a three-year after 11-15-26 in 50 with Seattle (Kimelman; Lane). Nazem Kadri (9 points in 16) and Nicolas Roy (5 in 15) get a full year. Gabriel Landeskog 14-21-35 in 60; Colorado 45-7-8 with him in the lineup (Maeir). Logan O’Connor played 13. Sakic, to The Denver Post (Aug. 23), called the group good enough and expected camp health. Artturi Lehkonen and Jared Bednar remain on expiring deals. Depth is younger and cheaper. It is not a one-for-one replacement of 100 combined points from the three traded forwards.

Schedule math: 84 games, not 82 (NHL PR, July 16). Two extra, more division. Last year’s 121 in 82 is 1.476 points per game — 124 if copied onto 84. The board is not pricing that. Maxwell’s split is the cleaner prior: 31-2-7 through 40 games (69 points), 24-14-4 the rest (52 points in 42). Second-half pace on 84 games is 104. Dallas finished the Central at 112 (Hockey-Reference). Minnesota was the other 100-point club at 104. BetOnline’s July 20 opens had Dallas 100.5 and Minnesota 101.5. Central still runs through Colorado. It does not run through 121.

Fair number currently sits at **109.5** (research-adjusted). Market 108.5. Raw gap: one point — the half-win line — before juice. Over -125 needs 55.6 percent. A 109.5 mean with ordinary season-total variance is not a spot of value at that price. Under -102 is a bet against the remaining 5-on-5 structure. No model overlay to average in. Pass. Do not lean it. Confidence 2/5. Shop the over only if the juice comes off; do not pay -125 to buy a one-point theory.
