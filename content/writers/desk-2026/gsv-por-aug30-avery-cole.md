Golden State still owns the league’s slowest pace and its best defense. It does not own Gabby Williams, Janelle Salaün, or Cecilia Zandalasini tonight. Those three are gone for the World Cup — official Valkyries preview, not a rumor — and they took about 66 minutes and 36 points of usage with them. Veronica Burton is the offense now (20.0 USG, 5.8 apg). Tiffany Hayes is the next usage piece in 16.5 minutes. That’s a different club than the one that hung 18 threes on Portland in June.

Portland is not whole either. Carla Leite’s contract was temporarily suspended Saturday (ESPN). That’s 15.9 points, 6.3 assists, 28.5 percent usage — the engine. Frieda Bühner is out the same way. Karlie Samuelson is questionable, left knee, from Friday in Atlanta. Megan DiLeo is the last swing: a Covers write-up had her already in Spain; the official IR and Nik Streng at OregonLive do not. She played 27 minutes Friday. Treat her as available until someone official says otherwise.

The rest picture is two-sided, not a free fade. Golden State has two full days after Thursday in Brooklyn — not a back-to-back — then Brooklyn-to-Portland and a three-hour time change at the end of a five-game road swing. Portland has one day after Atlanta and gets Moda. Slight home/rest edge for the Fire. Motivation sits the other way: Valkyries still chasing a 1-seed (28-11); Fire eliminated August 25, last game before a 17-day pause.

The spread has been -5.5 all day. Covers opened it there and nine books were still there at 3:51 p.m. ET. The public is on Golden State; the number hasn’t moved. Full-strength this is a bigger road favorite. Short-handed, with that travel, fair sits right on the price. Pass. Don’t manufacture a hook.

The total is the actual question. Live consensus is 158.5 -110 (157.5–159.5 shop range). Golden State’s last three: 80-66, 89-64, 79-60 — combined 146, 153, 139 — with the three who are now in Europe. Portland’s 101 in Atlanta included Leite’s 26. GSV is 1st in defensive rating, 15th in pace. Portland is 14th in DRtg and turns it over. Fair total sits closer to 153.5. Under 158.5 is the spot of value.
