# Green Bay at Minnesota, Week 1: the spread moved; Jacobs just did too

**By Casey Voss** · Kos Edge Analytics · NFC North desk
**Sport:** NFL
**Category:** Desk handicap
**Angle:** Minnesota −1.5 is a Murray-home number. Jacobs coming off the 53 is the part that still looks cheap.
**Date:** August 30, 2026
**Timestamp:** August 30, 2026 · afternoon ET
**Market fact-check:** August 30, 2026, afternoon ET · DraftKings via RotoWire; juice cross-checked on CLEATZ (Odds API live board)
**Market (DK / RotoWire, Aug. 30):** Vikings **−1.5** (−102) / Packers **+1.5** (−118); total **45.5** (−110); ML MIN −118 / GB −102
**Opening (DK Network, May 14):** Packers −1.5, total 44.5
**Sources:** Adam Schefter (ESPN); Rob Demovsky (ESPN); Tom Silverstein (Journal Sentinel); Kevin Seifert (ESPN); Alec Lewis (The Athletic); Jeremy Fowler (ESPN); Vikings.com; Packers.com; Ian Rapoport

Minnesota is 1.5. DraftKings, Sunday afternoon via RotoWire, posts Vikings −1.5 (−102) and Packers +1.5 (−118), total 45.5. FanDuel is the same spread at −105. The number flipped from Green Bay −1.5 in May. That flip is Kyler Murray. What dropped today is Josh Jacobs.

Jordan Love is healthy. Murray has been the starter since Aug. 11. The rest of this game is trench and backfield.

Micah Parsons is expected on reserve/PUP (ACL and meniscus, Week 15 2025). Tom Silverstein had him on PUP at camp. Ian Rapoport’s public target has been mid-October. Cutdown is still open (5 p.m. CT), so treat Week 1 out as expected, not a posted transaction. Jonathan Gannon then rushes with Lukas Van Ness, Barryn Sorrell, and Brenton Cox. That is not the defender Green Bay traded for.

Jacobs is not a Week 1 injury question. The NFL placed him on the commissioner’s exempt list Sunday (Adam Schefter, ESPN). He cannot practice or attend games. He does not count on the 53. The filed charges are misdemeanor battery and criminal damage to property. The list is administrative, not a ruling. It is enough to keep him out on Sept. 13.

Jeremy Fowler reported a trade for Kaleb Johnson from Pittsburgh. MarShawn Lloyd and Chris Brooks were already next. Lloyd has one career game. Johnson rushed for 69 yards as a rookie. PrizePicks still had Jacobs at 52.5 rushing yards after the exempt list posted. That is lag.

Tucker Kraft is expected “full go” Week 1 off the 2025 ACL (Rob Demovsky). Luke Musgrave is expected to stay PUP (neck). Aaron Banks is trending toward a start; LaFleur, via Silverstein, would not quite certify it. Right guard is not locked. Zach Bako-Bewele is back from the patellar.

Kevin O’Connell named Murray on Aug. 11 (Vikings.com; Kevin Seifert). A month of first-team work is why the spread crossed zero. His preseason was a cameo. J.J. McCarthy’s emergency status is not locked (mild deltoid ankle sprain vs. Baltimore, Seifert). Alec Lewis’s Athletic 53-man projection kept Murray and Carson Wentz and left McCarthy off.

Christian Darrisaw told Seifert he felt the best he has in two years. No named-beat Week 1 miss. Blake Brandel is the new center; snaps were messy in camp. Interior DL is youth after Minnesota let Javon Hargrave and Jonathan Allen walk. Hargrave is in Green Bay. Jamal Adams is on IR (torn quad; Rapoport, then O’Connell / AP). Harrison Smith is unsigned. Flores still has Dallas Turner and Andrew Van Ginkel. Justin Jefferson has no Week 1 designation.

Do not use the 16–3 Week 18 box. Love sat. Dome, weather N/A, sixteen days since the Aug. 28 finales. No short week.

A season-quality prior still lands near Minnesota −1.5. Love is the more settled quarterback. Home field covers that. Parsons being out has been in the market since camp. That prior is why the current number exists.

Research-adjusted, with Jacobs off the 53 today, the fair spread sits nearer **Minnesota −2.5**. That is one point. It is not half a win. The juice on the favorite is −102. The lean is Minnesota −1.5 while Jacobs rushing props are still on the board. Confidence is 2: the exempt list has no public end date, cutdown is not final, and kickoff is two weeks out. If the number goes to −2.5, the lean is gone.

The total is 45.5, up a point from May. Love can still throw. Murray’s first live start should not be a track meet. Fair sits nearer 44.5. One point on a Week 1 total is not a position. Pass. Do not dress that up as a soft Under.
