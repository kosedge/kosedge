# ARI: Allgeier listed RB1; Love usage still a committee — Camp News Break

**Timestamp:** August 6, 2026 · 4:00 PM ET  
**Team:** ARI  
**Category:** Camp News Break  
**Sources:** Josh Weinfuss (ESPN); Darren Urban / Cards Wire depth chart (Aug. 2); Arizona Sports; Mike LaFleur camp availability

Arizona’s first preseason depth chart listed **Tyler Allgeier as RB1** and No. 3 overall pick **Jeremiyah Love as RB2**, with James Conner third and Trey Benson behind him. That matches first-week practice texture: Allgeier has taken the majority of early first-team series; Love is mixing ones and twos and still drawing “he’s a rookie” coaching language. Conner has not taken 11-on-11 yet while working back from the ankle injury that ended 2025.

## Key points

- **Fact:** Draft capital on Love is not the same thing as Week 1 bell-cow volume. Early camp + depth chart say committee.
- **Context:** Consensus ADP that treats Love as an automatic RB1 from snap one is ahead of the grass. Allgeier’s early lead is earned-not-given under Mike LaFleur — not a lifetime ranking.
- **Market angle:** Cardinals win-total basement pricing already assumes offensive inconsistency. RB props and fantasy ranks should price a timeshare, not a monopoly, until Conner’s role and Love’s starter snaps stabilize.

## Usage shift / fantasy impact

Project a two- or three-back room into September: Allgeier early-down / script work, Love explosive and developmental snaps, Conner as veteran committee piece when cleared. Do not bury Love — No. 3 picks usually climb — but do not ignore the obvious: first-week reps and the published chart put Allgeier ahead. Benson’s burst in limited work is a depth note, not a starter claim yet.

## What changes the view

Love taking a clear majority of first-team carries across a padded week and a meaningful preseason script, or Conner clearing 11s and reclaiming early downs. Until one of those happens, KosEdge ranks Arizona RBs as committee with Allgeier’s early edge acknowledged.
