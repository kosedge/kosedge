# ATL: Penix ACL timeline + Tua flare-up pause the derby — Camp News Break

**Timestamp:** August 5, 2026 · 1:30 PM ET  
**Team:** ATL  
**Category:** Camp News Break

Atlanta opened Kevin Stefanski’s first camp without a true QB battle on the grass. Michael Penix Jr. is still limited to individual and 7-on-7 work after a Week 11 ACL tear — he told reporters it could take **up to four weeks** to reach the next clearance stage. Tua Tagovailoa missed openers with a back flare-up Stefanski and Schefter framed as short-term. Cooper Rush was signed so camp 11-on-11s had a veteran arm beside undrafted Jack Strand.

## Key points

- **Fact:** Neither contender has run a clean first-week competition; Penix’s medical timeline can push real 11-on-11 into late August.
- **Context:** Consensus already priced Falcons QB fog into a **6.5 / 7.5** win-total split. Injuries did not create the uncertainty — they froze the evaluation window.
- **Market angle:** Prefer shopping the board split over inventing a Week 1 starter lean. Season QB props stay Pass until one passer owns team periods for multiple practice weeks.

## Projected starter / fantasy impact

Public still wants a Penix-era ceiling or a Tua volume season. Camp says neither is earned yet. If Penix misses most of the preseason, Tagovailoa becomes the Week 1 favorite by availability, not by winning a derby. Fantasy WR/TE ranks tied to “full-season Penix” need a health asterisk; dual-QB model allocations should stay dual until clearance is real.
