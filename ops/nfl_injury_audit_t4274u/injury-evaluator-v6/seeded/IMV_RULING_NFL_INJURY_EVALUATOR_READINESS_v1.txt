      positive when home covers. Both arms are flipped, so the paired delta sign is flipped. This is
      inherited from the frozen score_rows L662-674 identity. Must be settled by acceptance T-1 with a hand-
      computed answer filed first. If confirmed: correct, and file the erratum for the formula.

      so (I-1 intends CLV against the closing line).
